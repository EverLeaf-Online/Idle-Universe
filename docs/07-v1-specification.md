# V1 Gameplay Specification

This document converts the high-level design into a concrete launch specification. It is the working definition of V1 unless playtesting or analytics justify a change.

## 1. V1 player fantasy

The player begins as a scavenger operating a single rusted recycler on Earth. During one run they grow into an industrial civilization capable of operating factories on multiple worlds and, finally, constructing a Reality Engine powerful enough to create a Singularity.

The visual escalation for one complete run is:

**Rusty Scrap Yard → Industrial Moon Base → Automated Mars Foundry → Deep-Space Industry → Solar Megastructure → Singularity**

The game should make this growth visible in the world, not only in menus or numbers.

## 2. Launch worlds

V1 ships with exactly five progression worlds.

| # | World | Theme | Primary gameplay purpose |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Earth Scrapyard | Salvage, recycling, primitive machinery | Tutorial and basic automation |
| 2 | Lunar Industrial Complex | Assembly lines and research | Introduces Research and production specialization |
| 3 | Mars Foundry | Robotics and high-energy production | Introduces stronger automation and machine synergy |
| 4 | Asteroid Nexus | Nanotechnology and antimatter | Introduces late-run scaling and expensive strategic upgrades |
| 5 | Solar Forge | Stellar-scale engineering | Final pre-prestige world and Singularity preparation |

Worlds are sequential for V1. The player cannot skip ahead during a fresh account's first runs.

### World unlock philosophy

Every world requires a combination of:

- Lifetime Credits produced during the current run,
- levels in one or more machines,
- a specific Research node,
- completion of the previous world's objective.

Exact numbers are Phase 2 balance values and are intentionally not locked in this document.

## 3. Launch machines

V1 ships with ten machines, two per world.

| Tier | Machine | World | Produces | Design role |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Scrap Recycler | Earth Scrapyard | Credits | First automated generator; teaches the core loop |
| 2 | Industrial Smelter | Earth Scrapyard | Credits | First expensive production choice and first cross-machine synergy |
| 3 | Assembly Line | Lunar Industrial Complex | Credits | Mid-early production engine; unlocks production-chain research |
| 4 | Research Lab | Lunar Industrial Complex | Research | Dedicated source of Research currency |
| 5 | Robot Factory | Mars Foundry | Credits | Unlocks automation-themed Research and visibly deploys robots |
| 6 | Fusion Reactor | Mars Foundry | Credits | High-output machine with strong milestone jumps |
| 7 | Nano Fabricator | Asteroid Nexus | Credits | Scales from Research bonuses and late-run efficiency nodes |
| 8 | Antimatter Reactor | Asteroid Nexus | Credits | Expensive late-run machine with very high output |
| 9 | Dyson Foundry | Solar Forge | Credits | Stellar-scale generator and penultimate production engine |
| 10 | Reality Engine | Solar Forge | Singularity progress / Credits | Final run objective; enables Singularity when requirements are met |

### Machine upgrade dimensions

All production machines use a common framework:

- **Level** — primary repeated purchase.
- **Output** — increased by level and modifiers.
- **Speed** — improved through Research/Core upgrades, not separate per-click micromanagement.
- **Milestones** — large bonuses at configured levels.
- **Evolution state** — visual upgrade at major milestones.

For V1, machine-level purchasing is the primary repeated action. Avoid separate speed/output/capacity buttons for every machine because that creates unnecessary UI noise.

### Standard milestone levels

Use these initial milestone anchors where appropriate:

**10 → 25 → 50 → 100 → 250**

Not every machine needs to reach every milestone before Singularity. Milestone rewards are configuration-driven.

## 4. Manual interaction

Manual earning exists only during onboarding and as an optional short-term activity.

### Earth opening

1. Pick up a piece of Scrap.
2. Drop/feed it into the Scrap Recycler.
3. Receive first Credits.
4. Purchase automated Recycler operation.
5. Manual feeding becomes optional rather than mandatory.

After the first few minutes, the game should be driven primarily by automation, upgrades, Research, and progression decisions.

## 5. Currency rules

### Credits

- Main in-run currency.
- Produced by production machines.
- Used for machines, machine levels, world unlocks, and some run upgrades.
- Reset on Singularity.

### Research

- In-run strategic currency.
- Primarily generated by the Research Lab.
- Also awarded by selected quests and world milestones.
- Used in the run Research tree.
- Reset on Singularity in V1.

### Singularity Cores

- Permanent prestige currency.
- Earned only by completing a Singularity.
- Spent in the permanent Core tree.
- Never reset by normal Singularity.

## 6. Run Research tree

V1 Research contains four branches. The exact numeric values are balance parameters; the structure and intent are locked.

### Production branch

1. **Efficient Motors** — global machine output increase.
2. **Refined Processing** — Recycler/Smelter/Assembly bonuses.
3. **Parallel Manufacturing** — stronger machine milestone bonuses.
4. **High-Energy Industry** — Fusion/Antimatter bonuses.
5. **Stellar Engineering** — required for Solar Forge progression; boosts Dyson Foundry.

### Logistics branch

1. **Conveyor Optimization** — early production bonus and visual conveyor speed increase.
2. **Orbital Logistics** — required to unlock Lunar-to-Mars progression.
3. **Autonomous Routing** — improves Robot Factory and reduces selected machine costs.
4. **Deep-Space Logistics** — required for Asteroid Nexus progression.
5. **Solar Supply Chain** — required for Solar Forge progression.

### Science branch

1. **Applied Metallurgy** — increases Research Lab efficiency and early machine output.
2. **Robotics** — unlocks Robot Factory's full automation bonus.
3. **Nanotechnology** — unlocks Nano Fabricator.
4. **Antimatter Containment** — unlocks Antimatter Reactor.
5. **Reality Theory** — unlocks Reality Engine.

### Efficiency branch

1. **Bulk Purchasing** — small machine-cost reduction.
2. **Energy Recovery** — Fusion and later-machine efficiency bonus.
3. **Research Optimization** — Research production increase.
4. **Industrial Compression** — global late-run cost reduction.
5. **Singularity Preparation** — improves Reality Engine progress and Singularity reward preview.

### Research design rules

- Branches have prerequisites, but players should make meaningful choices about order.
- At least one Research node is required for each world transition after Earth.
- Some nodes improve visuals or automation, not only raw multipliers.
- Research resets each Singularity so permanent Core upgrades can make rebuilding the tree progressively faster.

## 7. World objectives

Each world has a clear completion objective so progress is not defined only by a currency threshold.

### Earth Scrapyard

Objective: automate the Recycler and establish a functioning Smelter line.

### Lunar Industrial Complex

Objective: activate the Research Lab and complete Orbital Logistics.

### Mars Foundry

Objective: deploy the Robot Factory, activate Fusion power, and complete Robotics.

### Asteroid Nexus

Objective: stabilize antimatter production and complete Antimatter Containment.

### Solar Forge

Objective: build the Dyson Foundry and Reality Engine, then fill the Singularity progress requirement.

## 8. Singularity prestige

### Eligibility

A player may trigger Singularity only when all of the following are true:

- Solar Forge is unlocked.
- Reality Engine is built.
- Reality Engine reaches the required minimum level.
- The run meets the minimum run-value threshold.
- The Singularity progress meter reaches 100%.

Exact thresholds are Phase 2 balance values.

### On Singularity

Reset:

- Credits,
- Research,
- machine levels,
- run Research,
- world progression,
- run quests tied to progression.

Preserve:

- Singularity Cores,
- permanent Core tree,
- achievements,
- cosmetics,
- lifetime statistics,
- monetization entitlements,
- collection data,
- settings.

### Reward preview

The Singularity screen must show the player:

- Cores earned if they prestige now,
- next Core threshold,
- key systems that will reset,
- permanent bonuses that will remain.

Never make a reset ambiguous.

## 9. Permanent Core tree

V1 ships with four permanent branches.

### Industry

- **Core Output I** — global production bonus.
- **Core Output II** — additional global production bonus.
- **Machine Mastery** — stronger milestone bonuses.
- **Genesis Industry** — improved starting production after prestige.

### Knowledge

- **Research Memory I** — Research gain bonus.
- **Research Memory II** — larger Research gain bonus.
- **Fast Theory** — lowers selected Research costs.
- **Scientific Legacy** — begin each run with a small Research grant.

### Automation

- **Starting Automation** — Recycler automation begins unlocked after enough investment.
- **Auto Purchase** — optional automated purchase rules for selected machines.
- **Auto Upgrade** — automatic milestone-oriented purchasing toggle.
- **Advanced Automation** — improved automation controls/priority settings.

### Offline

- **Offline Efficiency I** — increases offline earnings percentage.
- **Offline Capacity I** — increases offline cap.
- **Offline Efficiency II** — further offline efficiency.
- **Offline Capacity II** — further offline cap.

Core costs and exact bonuses are Phase 2 values.

## 10. Offline progression

V1 rules:

- Free players always receive useful offline progress.
- Base offline cap target: 2 hours.
- Offline reward is based on a trusted server-side snapshot of eligible production.
- Returning players receive one concise claim/summary screen.
- Offline rewards do not advance one-time world unlock sequences or duplicate quest claims automatically.
- Permanent upgrades and paid entitlements may improve cap/efficiency without making the free system worthless.

## 11. Guided quest chain

V1 includes a deterministic onboarding/progression quest chain. Initial sequence:

1. Collect your first Scrap.
2. Earn your first Credits.
3. Automate the Scrap Recycler.
4. Upgrade Recycler to level 10.
5. Build the Industrial Smelter.
6. Unlock Lunar Industrial Complex.
7. Build the Assembly Line.
8. Activate the Research Lab.
9. Purchase your first Research node.
10. Unlock Mars Foundry.
11. Build the Robot Factory.
12. Activate Fusion power.
13. Reach Asteroid Nexus.
14. Stabilize the Antimatter Reactor.
15. Reach Solar Forge.
16. Build the Reality Engine.
17. Complete your first Singularity.

After the first Singularity, the guided chain gives way to repeatable objectives and achievements.

## 12. V1 achievements

Initial achievement set:

- Scrap Collector — collect first Scrap.
- Automated — automate first Recycler.
- Industrialist — own five machine types.
- Researcher — buy first Research node.
- Lunar Pioneer — unlock Lunar Industrial Complex.
- Red Planet Engineer — unlock Mars Foundry.
- Deep-Space Industrialist — unlock Asteroid Nexus.
- Star Builder — unlock Solar Forge.
- Millionaire — produce 1M lifetime Credits.
- Billionaire — produce 1B lifetime Credits.
- Machine Master — reach level 100 on any machine.
- First Singularity — prestige once.
- Repeat Universe — prestige 10 times.
- Core Engineer — purchase first permanent Core node.
- Fully Automated — unlock a major permanent automation node.

Reward values are defined during economy balancing.

## 13. Social V1

Keep social scope small.

Ship:

- players can visit another player's factory/plot in the same server,
- visible player name and Singularity count,
- one server-wide cooperative production target,
- basic lifetime/Singularity leaderboards.

Do not ship in V1:

- trading,
- guilds,
- PvP,
- complex gifting economies.

## 14. V1 UI screens

### Persistent HUD

- Credits,
- Research,
- income/sec,
- active objective,
- world/progression indicator,
- navigation buttons.

### Main screens

1. Machines
2. Research
3. Worlds
4. Singularity
5. Missions/Achievements
6. Shop
7. Settings

### Contextual panels

- machine purchase/upgrade panel,
- world unlock panel,
- offline-return summary,
- reward claim panel,
- Singularity confirmation,
- contextual monetization offer.

UI must be designed mobile-first and remain fully usable with mouse/keyboard.

## 15. First-session target flow

Initial target, subject to playtesting:

| Time | Target event |
|---|---|
| 0–30 sec | First Credits earned |
| 30–90 sec | Recycler automated |
| 2–4 min | Smelter built |
| 5–8 min | Lunar world unlocked |
| 8–12 min | Research introduced |
| 12–18 min | Mars unlocked |
| 18–24 min | Asteroid Nexus reached |
| 24–30 min | Solar Forge reached |
| 25–35 min | First Singularity |

This is a product hypothesis, not a promise. Phase 2 simulation and alpha data will determine the final curve.

## 16. Monetization placement rules for V1

Monetization follows the existing monetization document. For gameplay design specifically:

- no purchase prompt before the player understands automation,
- no required purchase to complete a normal Singularity,
- paid boosts accelerate progress but do not unlock an exclusive mandatory production tier,
- permanent passes should communicate their exact effect,
- developer-product currency packs should scale with current progression where practical,
- cosmetic opportunities should reinforce factory identity.

## 17. V1 content explicitly deferred

- subscriptions,
- season pass,
- trading,
- guilds,
- second prestige layer,
- large pet-equivalent system,
- full blueprint collection system,
- dozens of worlds,
- competitive PvP,
- user-generated factory marketplace.

## 18. Phase 1 definition of done

Phase 1 is considered complete when this specification is accepted as the implementation target and all systems above have clear ownership/dependencies in the technical roadmap.

The next phase is **Phase 2 — Economy Model**, which must assign concrete machine costs, outputs, Research costs, world gates, Core rewards, offline values, and simulate the path from a new account through multiple Singularities.
